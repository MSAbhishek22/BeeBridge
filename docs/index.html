How it works — in plain words

1. Bee listens. A small wearable remembers real conversations — the doctor, the pharmacist, the family call — only with consent.
2. BeeBridge tidies. It picks out the things that matter: medicines, appointments, promises, deliveries.
3. You just ask. Alexa+ answers in short, clear sentences. The day's summary waits on the TV in big, friendly letters.

How BeeBridge is different

Reminder apps make seniors type and schedule. BeeBridge needs no typing, no scheduling, no new habit — the memory is captured from conversations they were already having. Notes apps store text; BeeBridge answers questions. And the memory stays private, on your own computer — not in a company's cloud.
